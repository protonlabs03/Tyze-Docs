Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Where is my data stored?
A: All your transaction logs are stored strictly on your phone's internal storage. We do not store your data on external servers.

Q: What happens if I uninstall the app?
A: Since data is stored locally, uninstalling the app will permanently clear your transaction logs unless you exported a backup beforehand.

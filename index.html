Privacy Policy for Ledgr

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Ledgr We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Storage

Ledgr is built as a local-first, offline expense tracking application.

  • Local On-Device Storage: All financial records, expense categories, notes, and budgets created inside the App are saved exclusively on your local device within the App's isolated sandbox environment.
  • No Remote Servers: We do not operate external databases or servers for this App. Your personal logs are never uploaded or transmitted over the internet.
  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect, harvest, or request names, email addresses, phone numbers, location data, or device IDs.

2. Device Permissions & File Access

  • Internal Storage Access: By default, the App only accesses its own private internal storage directory managed directly by the Android Operating System.
  • External File Access (Optional): If you choose to export your expenses (e.g., CSV export), the App requests permission strictly to save that file to your local downloads.

3. Financial and Banking Data

Ledgr is a manual tracking utility. We do not connect to bank APIs, process payments, or read bank SMS messages automatically.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions, visit our Support Page or reach out to us via email.
